<TabItem label="Unreal Engine">

In Flair for Unreal Engine, this effect comes from the `Flair_Pigment_Density` effect layer in `/Flair/Effects`. Add it to the **Effect Layers** of a Flair Style actor; the effect's parameters then appear under **Parameters** in the actor's Details panel. See [Breaking down the Flair Style Actor](/unreal/artist-guide/style-actor/) for how both work.

</TabItem>

#### Pigment Density

<span class="pill-link">Art-directable</span>

The concentration of pigments, giving the render either a diluted or a more saturated and darker look.

#### Paper Granulation

<span class="pill-link">Art-directable</span>

The accumulation of pigments on the valleys of the paper (canvas). Concentrates the pigments on the valleys and creates a more saturated and darker look.
