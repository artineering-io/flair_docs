In Flair for Unreal Engine, this effect comes from the `Flair_Line_Detection`, `Flair_Line_Dilation` and `Flair_Line_Drawing` effect layers in `/Flair/Effects`. Add them to the **Effect Layers** of a Flair Style actor; the effect's parameters then appear under **Parameters** in the actor's Details panel. See [Breaking down the Flair Style Actor](/unreal/artist-guide/style-actor/) for how both work.

The [Mix-and-Match Flair Effect Layers](/unreal/technical-artist-guide/mix-and-match/) walkthrough builds a lines style from these layers.
