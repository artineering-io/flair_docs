<TabItem label="Unreal Engine">

In Flair for Unreal Engine, this effect comes from the `Flair_Bleeding_2Pass` and `Flair_Bleeding_4Pass` effect layers in `/Flair/Effects`. Add them to the **Effect Layers** of a Flair Style actor; the effect's parameters then appear under **Parameters** in the actor's Details panel. See [Breaking down the Flair Style Actor](/unreal/artist-guide/style-actor/) for how both work.
