<TabItem label="Unreal Engine">

In Flair for Unreal Engine, this effect comes from the `Flair_Edge_Darkening` effect layer in `/Flair/Effects`. Add it to the **Effect Layers** of a Flair Style actor; the effect's parameters then appear under **Parameters** in the actor's Details panel. See [Breaking down the Flair Style Actor](/unreal/artist-guide/style-actor/) for how both work.

</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Maya">

Edge darkening accumulates the pigments (color) gradually towards the edges, generating darker edges.

* **Simple** - Simple control over edges defined by the colors and depth.
* **Advanced** - Advanced control over edges defined by lightness, chromacity and depth. We recommend working with this for better results.

#### Edge Darkening Intensity

<span class="pill-link">Art-directable</span>

Strength of the edge darkening effect. A higher intensity will concentrate more color on the edges.
